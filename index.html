About

From RoK-3 to RoK-4 humanoid robotics

I have worked on humanoid robot platforms from RoK-3 to RoK-4, covering mechanical design, model-based walking control using WBC and MPC, dynamic motion planning, and mechanism-aware reinforcement learning.

My recent research focuses on RoK-4, a humanoid robot equipped with the ADAPT Hip Pitch–Knee differential mechanism, and reinforcement learning-based walking control validated through real-world experiments and full-mechanism simulation.

Projects

Research Projects

RoK-4

RoK-4 Humanoid Robot

A humanoid robot platform for high-performance bipedal locomotion using a Hip Pitch–Knee differential leg mechanism.

Mechanism-Aware RL Walking

A PPO-based walking controller using mechanism-aware observations, actions, and actuator–joint mapping.

ADAPT Differential Mechanism

An Advanced Differential and Agile Parallel Transmission mechanism for humanoid robot legs, coupling actuator inputs with Hip Pitch and Knee outputs.

Single-Leg Jumping Motion Planning

Dynamic motion planning for agile legged motion using nonlinear trajectory optimization and kinodynamic planning.

RoK-3

RoK-3 Humanoid Robot

Early humanoid walking experiments, robot platform development, and biped-to-full-humanoid integration.

Model-Based Humanoid Walking

Model-based walking experiments for humanoid robots, including torque-control-based biped walking.

Whole-Body and Model Predictive Control

Whole-body control and model predictive control for balance, support transition, and walking control.

Foot Design

Foot mechanism design, variable stiffness sole research, and terrain adaptation for uneven terrain and slope walking.
